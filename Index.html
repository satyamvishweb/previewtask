Jal Jeevan Mission (Rural)

Har Ghar Nal Se Jal

Providing Functional Household Tap Connections (FHTC) to every rural household across all 75 revenue districts.

  • 100% saturation for rural primary schools & Anganwadi centres.
  • Village Water & Sanitation Committee (VWSC) community participation.
  • Real-time solar pump and IoT sensor pressure monitoring.
Project Guidelines & DPR →

AMRUT 2.0 (Urban Water)

Universal Piped Supply in Towns

Ensuring universal coverage of drinking water supply and scientific sewerage networks in municipal corporations.

  • Circular water economy through treated sewage reuse for gardening/industry.
  • Restoration and de-silting of historic urban water bodies and lakes.
  • Implementation of DMA smart bulk flow metering to cut water losses.
Urban Project Framework →

Namami Gange Project

Pollution Abatement & River Basin

Intercepting untreated drain discharges and setting up advanced Sewage Treatment Plants along the Ganga & Yamuna river basins.

  • Construction of multi-stage biological nutrient removal (BNR) STPs.
  • Interception & Diversion (I&D) networks preventing river pollution.
  • 24x7 SCADA online effluent quality telemetry directly connected to CPCB.

Mandate & Constitution

About Uttar Pradesh Jal Nigam

Constituted in 1975 under the Uttar Pradesh Water Supply and Sewerage Act, U.P. Jal Nigam is the premier statutory engineering organisation responsible for the development and regulation of water supply and sewerage services across the state.

Statewide Execution

Planning, survey, design, and commissioning of macro-level water supply grids and sewerage works.

Ecological Preservation

Operating over 350 wastewater treatment plants to rejuvenate major river basins and local wetlands.

Citizen Accountability

Ensuring transparent service level standards under the Right to Information and Citizen's Charter.
